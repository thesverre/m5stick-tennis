Ready to run

  1. Put the stick in a front trouser pocket (a snug one) and stand facing the screen, with some clear floor around you.
  2. When the run starts, stand still for 2 seconds while the stick learns how it sits, then do one squat so it learns which way you face.
  3. Orange obstacles are low: jump. A small hop is enough. Blue ones are high: squat until you're under. Long ones need you to stay down.
  4. Red barriers block part of the road: take a quick step (or a small hop) to the left or right to move one lane over, and step back to return.
  5. Coins lie along the lanes between obstacles: step into their lane to pick them up, and jump for the ones in the air.
  6. Run on the spot, lifting your knees, to go faster: obstacles cleared at speed score more. Stand still and your runner eases back down to a jog.
