Grab your racket

  1. Hold the stick in your fist like a racket grip, top end toward the racket head, screen facing the same way as your palm (screen = strings).
  2. Point it at your screen and press the side button to line the racket up.
  3. Meet the ball as it reaches the white ring. You stay put: every ball comes to your forehand or backhand.
