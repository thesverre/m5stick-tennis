Red Light, Green Light

  1. Put the stick in a snug front trouser pocket and stand facing the screen.
  2. When the game starts, stand still for 2 seconds while the stick learns how it sits.
  3. Green light: the doll sings with her back to you (“mugunghwa kkochi pieotseumnida”, from a Korean children's game). Run on the spot, lifting your knees, to cross the field.
  4. Red light: she turns around. Freeze. If she sees you move, you lose a life and are sent back.
  5. Reach the red finish line before the time runs out. Then the next round starts, a little harder, and your lives carry over.
